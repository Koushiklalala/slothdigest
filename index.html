Step 1: Message Normalizer

Cleans incoming unformatted chat streams (WhatsApp, Slack, Discord). Extracts timestamps, participant handles, and separates body content from metadata.

Step 2: Noise Pruner Tool

Invokes Mastra's \`NoisePrunerTool\` to discard 80-90% of chat payload: automated bot status pings, reaction emojis, and redundant filler.

Step 3: Drama Suppression

Executes sentiment conflict evaluation. Distinguishes between critical problems and emotional human venting, preventing stress transference.

Step 4: Gemma 2 Reasoning

Prompt-engineered inference on Gemma 2 open weights. Formats the strict 2-line sloth briefing and ranks tasks by minimum human effort.
